Help & Support

How can we help you?

We're here to assist you with any queries or issues.

Popular Topics

Policy related queries Learn about policies, coverage, benefits and more.
You can view your active policies in the Farmers section or the Dashboard. For detailed policy guidelines, benefits, and exclusions, refer to the policy document provided to you during registration. If you need a copy of your policy document, please contact your coordinator.
Premium payment Learn about payments, due dates, receipts and failed transactions.
Premium payments are collected monthly or annually depending on your policy plan. You can pay via UPI, Bank Transfer, or Cash through your local Suraksha Didi. Always ask for a payment receipt and ensure it is updated in the app.
Claim process Understand how to raise a claim and track its status.
To initiate a claim, submit the claim request through the Claims tab. You will need to provide the Goat ID, health details, and a verification photo from your Suraksha Didi. Claims are typically processed within 7 working days after verification.
Policy details Know your policy status, duration, documents and renewals.
Each policy covers goat health treatment and secure life insurance. The duration is usually 1 year from the activation date. You can check the specific status, expiry date, and renewal options directly on the Goat details page.
Account & profile Manage your account, update profile and personal information.
Manage your personal profile, contact number, and login credentials from the Profile Settings tab. If you need to change your registered mobile number or role, please raise a request with the system administrator.
Other queries Can't find what you're looking for? We're here to help.
For other questions or technical support, please contact our helpline at +91 92373 92712 or chat with us. You can also write to support@ajafi.com, and our coordinator support team will resolve your issue within 24 hours.
